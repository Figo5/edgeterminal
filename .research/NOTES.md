# NFL prop settlement source notes

The six SportsGameOdds sportsbook labels are not bet slips. None records wager jurisdiction or special promotion eligibility. A grading result can therefore be a conditional research interpretation, not a confirmed sportsbook payout.

FanDuel and DraftKings require participation for ordinary player props in the sourced jurisdictions. FanDuel explicitly means a snap. DraftKings general text says participate; a football-specific source was inaccessible, so treat a confirmed snap as sufficient but an active nonparticipant as unresolved.

Bovada's ordinary Football Props section differs from its Prop Builder FAQ. The former makes an active nonparticipant action; the latter voids a nonparticipant. Until product type is known, active zero without a snap is unresolved.

BetMGM has a first-quarter injury protection condition, so an early exit cannot be uniformly settled from box-score stats. Caesars' accessible rule is Maryland retail only. ESPN BET is a historical brand and the accessible rule is a 2025 Massachusetts copy. For these labels, current settlement is unresolved without a current, applicable rule and bet slip.

Postponed and suspended games remain pending in research until an official final and the applicable book window is known. A canceled game has no player statistic. A push is statistical equality; refund treatment follows verified applicable terms only. Later statistical corrections are appended to research grades; they do not establish that the book resettled a wager.
