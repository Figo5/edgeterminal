## Recommendation and concrete blockers

Approve an **additive totals component** only after its evidence, identity, fallback, and evaluation contracts below are included in the Gate 1 plan. Do not implement before the user's Gate 1 approval. Keep the existing forecast policy and five-minute source/forecast/grading loop.

Directly modifying or copying the spread path has three concrete blockers:

1. **Spread-only validation cannot establish total coverage.** `nba_game_forecasts.validation_snapshots()` requires a spread grade and finite saved home spread (`nba_game_forecasts.py:518`, `:539`). A game with a valid saved total but no spread would disappear. Totals needs its own reader and eligibility rules; do not loosen the existing reader or invent a missing line.
2. **Editing shared sealed modules can invalidate another active model.** `nba_spread_learning.active()` requires exact equality with its serving code catalog (`nba_spread_learning.py:70–87`). That catalog includes spread model/data/market/learning, game forecasts/context, NBA baseline, pricing, analytics, source/identity, and the pinned manifest. `nba_model_manifest.py` separately pins the active NBA prop artifact and baseline/prop/pricing source. Preserve these files and old pins byte-for-byte. A totals change must not be implemented by repinning an existing artifact, changing an old version's meaning, or causing a spread/prop rollback.
3. **Raw possession counts are not yet regulation pace.** `nba_spread_data.box_features()` computes full-game estimated possessions (`:216–249`), while `nba_data.parse_event()`/`parse_summary()` do not return duration or overtime count (`nba_data.py:114–225`). A pace model needs its own verified duration transform before accepting overtime games as pace evidence.

The audit's 121 resolved totals and November 4 results are inspected development evidence, not a new outer holdout. The audit alone cannot authorize a fixed bias subtraction or a new active total model.

## Missing data and overtime contract

Keep forecasts and picks for every game supported by the incumbent. An absent total quote affects actual-line grading, not the ability to project. Insufficient current-season rows, possession coverage, duration, opponent evidence, or an unavailable/rejected total artifact must select an explicit, deterministic retained-total fallback; do not drop the game or substitute zero possessions. Record counts, season composition, effective weights, available/missing feature flags, and the exact fallback reason. Validate coverage and accuracy on the common supported universe, including fallback games.

Treat optional evidence that is absent as missing; treat contradictory or corrupted evidence as an integrity failure. Quarantine the bad source or total component and preserve a previously verified incumbent/fallback where possible, with a visible error. Do not silently continue with the corrupt row or let one game's failure block other forecasts or grades.

The overtime contract must distinguish full-game possession estimates from possessions per 48 regulation minutes. Bind duration/overtime to explicit archived final-period or period-by-period evidence, with team/event/score reconciliation; if that evidence is absent or inconsistent, withhold that pace feature. For a documented `n` overtime periods, the pace normalization may use `raw possessions * 48 / (48 + 5*n)`. Efficiency must use points and possessions on the **same** duration basis; dividing full-game points by already normalized possessions inflates efficiency. Preserve raw values and the normalization method. The forecast target and grading label remain the actual full-game total including overtime. Any learned expected overtime contribution requires earlier fitting/calibration evidence, not a correction to historical grades.

Do not silently activate the baseline's existing all-rows-have-possessions branch. Season-aware scoring, pace, offense/defense efficiency, opponent adjustment, and uncertainty are separate candidate effects that must be selected on earlier data. A convex blend toward the identical saved line cannot by itself reverse a pick's side; do not claim a totals win-rate fix from numerical shrinkage alone.

Do not route it through the `NBA` prop adapter or a shared NBA/NFL active-model slot. Keep existing NBA/NFL prop actions unchanged. Retain them.

`nba_ops.run()` refreshes forecasts and grading every 300 seconds and runs spread input dispatch afterward (`:804–818`). Totals may add bounded source collection/cheap checks and enqueue only after capture/grading. No fit, historical replay, calibration, all-source hashing, or large scan may run inside the source worker, a request/status read, the research lock, or a SQLite writer transaction. A long totals job can wait or fail while live refresh/grading continue.

Use the existing short nonblocking publication lock pattern (`learning_publication.py:36–61`): verify/replay expensive evidence outside locks; then recheck active totals event, exact forecast/grade/final-source heads, source descriptors, code seals, game eligibility, and consumption inside one short writer transaction. Include all fit/development/calibration/selection/validation dependencies, not only outer labels. An intervening correction, code change, competing activation, capture lock, or near NBA/NFL kickoff must defer or reject publication without replacing the active component. Candidate registration, whole-date consumption, publication proof, and the owning job checkpoint must commit atomically. A resumed job publishes the already sealed proposal without refitting or re-evaluating fresh alternatives against the spent holdout. Failed and rejected proposals retain their consumed outer dates.

## Chronological acceptance contract

Before fitting, freeze candidate families, preprocessing, uncertainty distribution/probability contract, metrics, numerical acceptance thresholds, tie/push rules, minimum dates/games, and the outer eligibility/consumption rule. Use chronological whole Eastern-date cohorts for fit, development, probability calibration, any model/feature selection, and a later outer validation; combine selection with development only if explicitly declared in advance. Never split two team rows, multiple revisions, or games from the same date across phases. A late prior label must purge/quarantine its entire date or delay the next phase, not create an outcome-selected partial date. Wait for incomplete outer dates rather than measuring a convenient resolved subset.

Historical reconstructed-score training is development evidence with explicit receipt assumptions; it is not proof of historical live availability. Finalize any historical core before the earliest next-phase forecast. Require each phase's actual label receipts before the earliest next-phase as-of. Outer outcomes must not select season weights, fallback thresholds, opponent features, bias corrections, probabilities, sigma, or promotion gates. Poisoning calibration may change only declared uncertainty parameters; poisoning outer labels may change metrics/decision but none of the candidate parameters.

Reserve new, uninspected complete dates after the agreed design/freeze boundary. Exclude the audited season through November 4 and reconcile prior consumed/inspected cohorts before assigning an outer window. At minimum, maintain totals consumption by canonical game **and entire Eastern date**, independent of version/family renaming; define cross-component reuse explicitly and exclude any date whose outcomes already influenced totals choices. Used outer games can become later training once chronologically eligible, but never become a fresh outer test. Consume rejected evaluations too, including pre-ledger proposals.

No weaker sample floor than the spread path's 60 whole games across seven distinct dates should be adopted without an explicit rationale; this is a floor, not assurance of adequate statistical power. Compare the new total with the exact saved incumbent total and the same saved ESPN total line on predeclared eligible rows. Require full supported-game prediction coverage and predefined material improvements in bias/MAE, uncertainty reliability/Brier, and no unacceptable actual-line W–L regression, with paired whole-date uncertainty. Record pushes separately and use a declared conditional no-push probability. Report RMSE, interval coverage, over/under direction, version/date/coverage strata, missing lines, and pending/excluded labels. Do not manufacture a market pick at its own mean or claim these saved lines are closing prices.
