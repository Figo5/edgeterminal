## Findings and limits

1. **Totals waiting is an evidence-boundary issue, not proof that its numerical family is wrong.** `nba_totals_model.fit_candidate` allocates the latest whole dates to 120 calibration and 120 development examples, then puts the rest in fit. Only after partitioning does it reject boundaries where an earlier phase's maximum actual label or selected-feature receipt is at or after the later phase's earliest forecast as-of. `nba_totals_data.training_examples` deliberately labels historical reconstruction as `reconstructed_at_cutoff`; it carries real receipt timestamps. Imported old event dates therefore do not establish that their inputs or results were available to a later historical phase.
2. A read-only query on November 9 found the latest totals run, ID 54, waiting with 3,672 examples and `NBA totals phase date/label receipt chronology conflict`; there were **zero registered totals candidates**. Earliest successful team-history receipt was October 20 13:18:26Z; spread-team-history bootstrap receipt October 29 13:27:03Z; final-boxscore receipt October 20 23:02:36Z. Aggregate receipt ranges alone cannot establish exactly how many complete phases can be formed. A receipt-aware diagnostic must compute this before promising the candidate can be fitted immediately.
3. **The NBA family already includes the desired first-order bias correction.** Its fixed 20-member family is incumbent/intercept plus season and pace methods over three shrinkages and three recent weights. Fit learns the intercept; earlier development selects the method; calibration estimates residual sigma. The November 2–8 bias is a development hypothesis and does not justify a hard-coded subtraction or enlarging the candidate family after viewing validation.
4. **NFL production game forecasts are separate from the existing NFL prop learner and archived linear comparison.** Serving flows through `engine.weekly_board` to shared `analytics.football.project_game`; the numerical base is also used by other paths. Prefer an additive NFL spread-only component and explicit version identity. Do not change shared football constants, CFB behavior or prop parameters to repair NFL spread performance.
5. A convex blend of model margin `m` with the exact implied market margin `q=-home_line`, `m' = a*m + (1-a)*q`, satisfies `m'-q = a*(m-q)` for `a>0`. It cannot change the selected side against that same line. It can improve point MAE or probability calibration; those improvements alone cannot be described as better pick accuracy. A genuine side-changing candidate needs a separately learned residual/context feature or other independently validated correction.

## Follow-up: staged totals learning and NFL provenance

The specialized planners' subsequent inspection identifies a genuine data gap, not merely a feasible split missed by the allocator. The safe path is to stage the existing finite NBA family: freeze fitting evidence/cutoff and all 20 intercept-fitted methods now, collect at least 120 future whole-date development examples to choose the method, then at least 120 later whole-date calibration examples, then the existing 60 unused future-game/seven-date outer gate. The 660 fit and multiple-season requirements remain. No family expansion or new hand-picked bias correction is needed to get this process started.

This changes the learner lifecycle materially and needs an explicit versioned stage ledger. The following are required:

- A once-sealed fit stage must persist across nightly retries; do not roll its fitting cutoff forward every day while pretending to collect later phases. Stage transitions occur once under checked ownership and persist a content hash, eligible whole-date universe, actual receipts and predecessor stage identity.
- Development forecasts for every predeclared method must be stored before physical tipoff using only actual available inputs and the same incumbent/source contract. A method cannot be retrospectively evaluated from a new history snapshot after the target final. When development ends, freeze the method before calibration forecast capture; freeze calibration sigma before outer capture. Full-family development calculation must remain cheap and bounded in capture.
- A delayed source or correction that crosses a phase boundary cannot be backdated or silently replace a sealed phase. Preserve original frozen evidence; revalidate its dependency heads before transition/publication. Report stale/conflicting coverage and define a conservative, append-only terminal/deferred transition. Do not silently rerun fitting on development/calibration/outer outcomes under a new stage name.
- Development and calibration counters may exceed 120 to finish a whole date. They cannot stop early based on favorable outcomes or skip an inconvenient supported game. Required missing results keep a whole date pending. All unexposed outer cohorts remain globally reserved/consumed exactly as before.
- No model can be declared improved, ready or activated merely because the staged pipeline starts making progress. UI must distinguish collecting development, collecting calibration, validating and live active version.
