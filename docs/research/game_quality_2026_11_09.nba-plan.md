## Confirmed failure and coverage limit

`nba_totals_model.fit_candidate` sorts examples by event date, assigns the newest whole dates until calibration has at least 120 examples, the preceding dates until development has 120, and every earlier date to fit. It then correctly requires both labels and reachable feature evidence from the earlier phase to have been received before the next phase's earliest prediction as-of. Those conditions cannot hold for the selected historical phases: the source bodies were acquired in October 2026, after many reconstructed March/April 2026 games.

Current status reports 3,672 examples, zero totals candidate freezes and no active adaptive totals version. Daily re-running the same date-only partition cannot create missing chronological evidence; later corrections can move a receipt maximum further forward.

A bounded metadata-only read during this planning turn found 146 current-season NBA schedule entries through Nov 8, and only 78 after the current all-history fit prefix's Oct 29 receipt maximum. A future implementation must verify such a prefix from actual source bytes, true receipt times and the required two seasons/660 fit examples. The planning metadata inventory does not prove that any particular earlier prefix is sufficient.

## Integrity and recovery rules

- Resolve source corrections at each sealed receipt cutoff using the existing strict source identity/hash/duration/equal-clock guards. Later bodies cannot change an earlier sealed fit/feature bank. Corrections that invalidate a stage require an append-only explicit invalidation and bounded fresh stage, not an in-place rewrite or concealed fallback.
- Freeze complete eligible date membership without outcomes, fail on unexplained omitted supported games, and distinguish genuinely unsupported feature/line coverage from missing finals. No accuracy-driven exclusion.
- Freeze before physical tipoff and verify source receipts at actual as-of; passed logical `now` cannot manufacture pregame evidence. No future label/feature receipt crosses a phase boundary.
- Preserve lease ownership, idempotency and compare-and-swap checkpoint semantics. A crash during source verification, coefficient creation, stage sealing, a final-date transition or publication resumes the identical sealed inputs or records an explicit failure; it never reruns selection on outer data or discards a consumed holdout.
- Keep heavy work outside capture/grading locks. Retain socket denial/credential removal and bounded resource limits of the existing offline worker. Match portable rooted path/hash checks after `/app` relocation.
