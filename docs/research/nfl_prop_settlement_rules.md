## Evidence required for a real grade

`final_game_source` and `player_stat_source` each require source name, retrieval time, existing source file or immutable game-grade DB row, and SHA-256 verified against its exact bytes. The exact source bytes are retained once in an append-only content-addressed table. A final game also requires both official scores. Zero or inactive players additionally require `participation_evidence` with its own source/time/file/hash plus explicit game-day active-roster and played-snap values. An absent player row is pending, never a fabricated zero. Postponed and suspended games remain pending. Evidence lacking any required field fails before append.

The original prop freeze cannot be updated or deleted. New official-stat values append a grading revision and keep the earlier grade. After a stat correction, the book interpretation becomes `undetermined` unless actual resettlement evidence is available. A change to the rules file changes its hash and appends a new grading revision with the old and new rule snapshots still auditable. The report keeps retrospective validation and prospective 2026 results separate, and labels all book records as conditional interpretations.

Week 3 is one small forward sample. Strong or poor Week 3 results will not automatically retune point models or uncertainty, create a betting threshold, or promote picks. The live product remains `RESEARCH_ONLY` and PASS.
